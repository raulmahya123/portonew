01

Overview

Mine-Pro is a comprehensive, end-to-end operational planning and execution platform designed specifically for the rigorous demands of modern open-pit mining.

It serves as the central nervous system for site operations, seamlessly connecting field sensors, heavy machinery telemetry, and human reporting into a unified, actionable data ecosystem.

By bridging the gap between raw data extraction and high-level strategic planning, Mine-Pro ensures operational resilience, maximizes fleet utilization, and provides real-time visibility across all phases of the mining lifecycle.

02

Business Context & Problem

Inefficiency Detected

Prior to Mine-Pro, operations suffered from severe data fragmentation. Critical information regarding fleet coordination, material movement, and maintenance schedules was siloed across legacy spreadsheets, disparate vendor software, and manual paper trails.

warning

Fragmented Telemetry

Inability to cross-reference machine health with production targets in real-time.

schedule

Delayed Decision Making

Lagging indicators resulted in reactive maintenance rather than predictive planning.
